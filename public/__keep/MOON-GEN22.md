# Moon water — gen22 storyboard

After the quiet sun. The same lake. No words on the paint.
Figures stay far. No new face. Meet the stone. Do not take it.

The five stills sit at the end of the present: moon, shore, path, lantern, stone.
The rest are the board. Swipe right through them. North rises. South delves.
The middle of the stone opens the restart puzzle.
