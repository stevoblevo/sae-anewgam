# Semiotic — color is meaning

Few words. The picture carries the law.

| mark | color | means | never means |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| peach | warm pink-gold | walk together, sisters, her | a prize |
| mint | cool green | rest, well, pink-inside | a lock |
| orange | carrot, door-like | meet, offer, like | chopped, scored |
| red | rain wash | weather | fall, reign-as-crown |
| gold | play dot, lantern | tap play, light | autoplay |
| obsidian | black glass | the way when light goes down | a villain |
| white | ring, lace | mark | empty |
| lilac | blossom path | you came | a collectible |

Fawn / deer = beside.
Whole carrot = gift.
Leaf = not a trophy.
Gold dot on a tile = this picture can move. Tap Play. Six seconds. Still returns.

Image comms for ourselves use these marks only.
No QR. No URL. No private path.
