# Easy immerse touch

One plate. Full bleed. Thumb first.

Swipe
- Need ~64px sideways and more X than Y
- Vertical rest does not skip
- Left fifth = back. Right fifth = next. Center is the picture.

Marks
- Beads along the bottom. Gold = here.
- Play is a gold circle ≥44px, bottom-right, only if motion exists
- Film mark returns home. Wall mark opens the room.

Play
- Tap starts. Tap or Esc stops.
- Six seconds. No loop. Still returns.
- Motion never advances the potato.

Not on the film
- Pinch zoom
- Auto-advance
- Camera

Wall open
- Wheel / pinch zoom
- Hand pans
- Next only from marks or a clear sideways swipe at zoom 1
