# Restart

Entry: the peach dot, ways, restart. Or `#restart`.

The landing is the gaslight door. Restart clears the film receipts and shuffles the puzzle again.

Swipe down into the field. The field scrolls across and down. A tile beside the empty well slides in on a tap, or on a swipe toward the well.

The sun in the middle is the wordless plate. No sentence sits on it.

Solved, the door is first and the sun is center. Open returns to the present.
