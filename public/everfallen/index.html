Everfallen

a night garden, falling into other ground

Steer by dragging, moving the mouse, arrow keys, or a stick. Bloom with a tap, click, space, or the A button to pull petals in and break thorns. Catch the colour that is falling. Green cuts you until the knitted doll.

Rover (R, the Y button, or ◎) opens a Mars drive in a small window that keeps running beside the fall. Hold its right side to drive, left to reverse, or use the right stick or triggers. Every gold ring the doll reaches is luck for the fall.
